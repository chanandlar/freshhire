# FreshHire

**Job listings that are actually fresh.**

FreshHire shows you only the most recent job openings — nothing older than 7 days. If it's on FreshHire, it was posted recently.

---

## The Problem

Most job boards are cluttered with listings that are weeks or months old. You apply, and either get no response or find out the role was already filled. It's a waste of time for everyone.

## What FreshHire Does Differently

Every listing on FreshHire is from the last 7 days, sorted newest first. Stale postings are removed automatically. What you see is what's actually open right now.

---

## MVP

- Search jobs by keyword, location, country (US / India), job type, and salary range
- Results always sorted by most recently posted
- Covers openings across all industries and company sizes
- Click through directly to apply on the company's website
